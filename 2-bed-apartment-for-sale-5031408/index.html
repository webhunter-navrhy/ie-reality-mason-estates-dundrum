Mason Estates are delighted to bring to the market this stylish two-bedroom apartment in the sought after gated development of St James`s Wood. This apartment is set in a small block which holds a nice position within the complex and comes with a secure parking space. It would suit a first time buyer or an investor looking for an ideal investment which is not rent capped.

The well-proportioned accommodation measuring circa 68sqm/732sqft is very presented in good condition and comprises of an entrance hallway with utility/hot-press plumbed for washing, large living/dining room with the kitchen off, two bedrooms and the bathroom.

Ideally located on the tree lined South Circular Road close to the villages of Kilmainham and Rialto and with easy access to the City Centre. Just a short stroll from a host of local amenities including a choice of shops, post office, cafés, bars and restaurants, while recreational facilities such as the Irish Museum of Modern Art at Kilmainham Hospital, Phoenix Park and walks along the Grand Canal easily accessible. Nearby hospitals (within walking distance) include St James`s and the new National Children`s Hospital. There are excellent transport links with the Red LUAS line within minutes` walk, and the area is serviced by several regular public bus routes.
